# About

## Project

MooVision is a computer vision pipeline developed to automatically detect
cross-sucking behaviour in socially housed dairy calves from continuous barn
camera footage. The project was developed as a capstone project for the
[UBC Master of Data Science](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/) program
(2025–2026) in partnership with the
[UBC Animal Welfare Program](https://awp.landfood.ubc.ca/).

Cross-sucking occurs when one calf sucks on the body parts of another,
leading to physical injury, disease transmission, and reduced future milk
production. It is a key barrier to the wider adoption of social housing in
dairy farms. With Canada's updated Code of Practice for Dairy Cattle
requiring all indoor-housed calves to be socially housed by 4 weeks of age
beginning April 1, 2031, scalable tools for studying cross-sucking have
become increasingly important.

The pipeline reduces the reliance on manual behavioural annotation by
automatically detecting cross-sucking events in raw video, producing
structured event metadata and annotated clip segments for researcher review.

## Partner

**UBC Animal Welfare Program**
Faculty of Land and Food Systems, University of British Columbia

The UBC Animal Welfare Program conducts research on the behaviour, cognition,
and welfare of farm animals. This project was developed to support their
ongoing research into cross-sucking behaviour in socially housed dairy calves
at the UBC Dairy Farm.
